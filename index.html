⚖️ This is an automated screening aid. Text extraction from CVs can miss or misread details. Final eligibility must be decided by the scrutiny / selection committee against original certificates and the applicable regulations.

Regulations applied

  • UGC Regulations, 18 July 2018 on minimum qualifications for appointment of teachers in universities and colleges, with the 2nd Amendment, 2023: NET/SET/SLET is the minimum for direct recruitment of Assistant Professors (Ph.D. optional from 1 July 2023; Ph.D. holders exempt).
  • AICTE Regulations, 1 March 2019 for teachers in technical institutions: Engineering & Technology, MCA, Management.
  • Pay levels: 7th CPC revision for university and college teachers (MHRD, now the Ministry of Education), as adopted in the UGC 2018 and AICTE 2019 regulations.
  • The draft UGC Regulations, 2025 were released for consultation in January 2025. If they are notified, update rules.js.

Rules not checked automatically: relevance of the discipline, recognised university, regular-mode Ph.D., UGC research score (unless stated in the CV), age limits and reservation roster. Check these during scrutiny.
